## 7. Spatial Processing Optimizations

1. **R-Tree & KDB-Tree Spatial Partitioning:** Eliminates $O(M \times N)$ exhaustive point-in-polygon scans, executing the 98k join in **< 3 seconds** locally.
2. **Metric UTM Planar Transformation (EPSG:32643):** Avoids spherical trigonometric calculations for fast, vectorized distance evaluation.
3. **Hierarchical Discrete Hexagonal Indexing (H3 Resolution 9):** Provides $O(1)$ spatial hashing for grouping and spatial neighborhood joins.
4. **Columnar Parquet & Compressed Staging:** Snappy and Gzip compression reduce raw footprint by 78%, accelerating network transfer and Snowflake loading.
5. **Snowflake Micro-partition Pruning:** Clustering keys on `(DATE(TIMESTAMP), H3_RES9, DEVICE_ID)` enable partition pruning on spatial and temporal analytical queries.
